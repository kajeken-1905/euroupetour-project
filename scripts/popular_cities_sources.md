# Most visited cities 2024 (src/data/popularCities.ts)

Checked 2026-10-25. All figures are for 2024, the latest year with an official count for every city.
No single source covers all 18 (Euromonitor publishes arrivals only for its global top 10), so each is the city's
or country's official statistic. Most are foreign (non-resident) guests arriving at accommodation; where no such
count exists the method is noted.
